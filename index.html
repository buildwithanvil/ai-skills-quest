The Rules of The Forge

01
You must agree on the project together. If you can't agree, Dad picks.
02
You cannot do each other's tasks. Builder builds. Scholar architects. Stay in your lane, trust the other.
03
You must integrate your work. The project only counts if both halves connect and run together.
04
You demo to Dad together. Both present. Both answer questions. This is the boss battle.
05
If it breaks during the demo, explain why. Debugging earns more respect than a fake success.
